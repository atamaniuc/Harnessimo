# PROGRESS

State carried between sessions. Read this first; update it before you stop.

## Now

_What is being worked on, in a sentence. What is blocked, and on what. If nothing is
waiting on a human, say so — the absence of that sentence reads as an unanswered
question._

## Done

_Closed items, newest last. One line each: what now holds that did not before._

## Next

_The next two or three things, in order. Not a backlog — the queue is the backlog._

## Open questions for the human

_Decisions the loop may not take on its own: scope, licensing, direction. Empty is a
valid and good state._
